Our base recipe for 1.8 kg of Asian sous vide pork belly: a blended marinade of soy sauce, mirin, fish sauce, spring onion, garlic and ginger, an overnight marinade, then 8 hours at 76 °C. Finish thick slices until golden and crisp and serve with a sauce made from the cooking juices.

## Ingredients

- 1800 g pork belly {scale}
- 150 ml soy sauce {scale}
- 150 ml mirin {scale}
- 115 g granulated sugar {scale}
- 37 ml fish sauce, about 2½ tablespoons {scale}
- 3 spring onions {scale}
- 5 cloves garlic {scale}
- 35 g fresh ginger {scale}

## Instructions

1. **Day 1 — Blend the marinade.** Roughly chop the spring onions, garlic and ginger. Young ginger does not need peeling; washing it thoroughly is enough. Place all marinade ingredients in a blender or tall blending jug and blend until fairly smooth using a blender or immersion blender.
2. **Marinate overnight.** Place the pork belly and marinade in one or two vacuum bags. Seal and marinate in the refrigerator for 12–16 hours.
3. **Day 2 — Cook sous vide.** Preheat the water bath to 76 °C. Place the sealed bags containing the pork belly and marinade in the bath. Cook for 8 hours. Keep the meat fully submerged and leave enough space around the bags for water to circulate. Remove the bags from the bath when done.
4. **Chill and store for the next day.** Leave the pork belly in the sealed bags. Chill rapidly in a generous ice-water bath until the centre is cold too. Refrigerate at no more than 4 °C. The cold meat will be easy to cut into neat slices the next day.
5. **Day 3 — Prepare for finishing.** Remove the cold pork belly from the bags and reserve the cooking juices. Remove excess marinade, pat the meat thoroughly dry and cut into thick slices.
6. **Finish until crisp.** Sear the slices in a hot frying pan or briefly cook under a hot oven grill until golden and caramelised. Watch carefully: the sugar makes them prone to burning.
7. **Make the sauce.** Strain the reserved cooking juices and bring to a full boil. Reduce gently until lightly syrupy. Taste as you go: the soy sauce and fish sauce can make the sauce too salty if reduced too far.
8. **Serve.** Spoon a little sauce over the crisp pork belly and serve.

## Notes

- The base quantity is 1.8 kg of pork belly, divided here into 8 portions of about 225 g raw meat. Adjust the number of servings to suit the rest of the menu.
- Marinate for 12–16 hours in the refrigerator. Cook sous vide at 76 °C for 8 hours.
- The time estimate allows about 15 minutes of preparation, 8 hours sous vide, about 10 minutes of finishing and 16 hours of marinating. Extra time for chilling and storage until day 3 is not included; plan over three days if you want to slice the meat cold.
- Based on the [Anova sous vide pork belly recipe](https://recipes.anovaculinary.com/recipe/sous-vide-pork-belly), adapted to our quantities and method: blend the marinade and marinate overnight.
- We use 76 °C instead of the original 76.7 °C. This is our standard base recipe for Asian sous vide pork belly.
