An airy, velvety smooth foam of white chocolate and 10% Greek yoghurt with a subtle hint of citrus.

**Tools needed:** cream dispenser (0.5L) and 1 N2O cream charger.

## Components

### White chocolate yoghurt espuma

#### Ingredients
- 100 g white chocolate, finely chopped {scale}
- 120 ml heavy cream (min. 35% fat) {scale}
- 120 g Greek yoghurt (10% fat, at room temperature) {scale}
- Zest of ¼ organic lime or lemon {scale}
- ½ sheet of gelatine (soaked in cold water) {scale}
- Small pinch of salt

#### Instructions
1. Soak the half sheet of gelatine in plenty of cold water for 5 minutes.
2. Heat the heavy cream with the citrus zest and pinch of salt in a saucepan until just before boiling.
3. Remove from the heat, squeeze the water out of the gelatine and dissolve it in the hot cream.
4. Immediately add the finely chopped white chocolate. Let it sit for 1 minute, then whisk until it forms a smooth ganache.
5. Gradually whisk the 10% Greek yoghurt into the chocolate cream until smooth and uniform.
6. Pour the mixture **through a fine mesh sieve** directly into the siphon. Press with a spoon to extract all citrus oils, but hold back solid zest bits to prevent clogging the valve.
7. Close the siphon tightly and screw on **1 cream charger ($N_2O$)**.
8. Shake the siphon vigorously upside down 6 to 8 times.
9. Remove the charger holder and lay the siphon horizontally in the refrigerator for at least 1.5 to 2 hours.

### Serving

#### Ingredients
- Fresh red fruit (e.g. raspberries or strawberries) or shortbread crumbs
- Extra citrus zest for garnish

#### Instructions
1. Remove the siphon from the refrigerator about 10 minutes before serving (so the cocoa butter softens).
2. Just before using, shake briefly 2 to 3 times.
3. Hold the siphon vertically upside down and pipe a cloud of espuma over fresh fruit or cookie crumbs in a glass or coupe.
4. Garnish with some fresh citrus zest.
