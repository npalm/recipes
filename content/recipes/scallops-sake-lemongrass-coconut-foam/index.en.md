A refined Japanese-French starter for two: four golden seared scallop halves per person on warm, airy coconut foam with sake, lemongrass, ginger and lime. Fresh pomegranate seeds provide a bright contrast.

## Components

### Sake, Lemongrass and Coconut Foam

#### Ingredients

- 75 ml sake, reduced to about 40 ml {scale}
- 100 ml coconut milk {scale}
- 75 ml unsweetened whipping cream, about 35% fat {scale}
- 1 stalk fresh lemongrass {scale}
- 2 thin slices fresh ginger {scale}
- ½–1 tsp lime juice, fresh or thawed {scale}
- A small pinch of salt
- N₂O charger for the siphon, according to the manufacturer's instructions

#### Instructions

1. Bruise the lemongrass with the back of a knife and cut into pieces. Place the sake, lemongrass and ginger in a small saucepan.
2. Bring gently to the boil and reduce until about 40 ml of liquid remains. If reduced a little too far, top up to 40 ml with a little sake or water.
3. Add the coconut milk and cream. Heat gently to about 70–80 °C and infuse over low heat for 5 minutes without boiling vigorously.
4. Remove from the heat and strain very carefully through a fine sieve, lined with cheesecloth if needed, so no lemongrass fibres remain.
5. Start with ½ teaspoon of lime juice and a small pinch of salt. Taste and add more lime juice if needed, up to about 1 teaspoon in total. The flavour should be subtle: gently aromatic from lemongrass and ginger, creamy from coconut and fresh from lime, without pronounced acidity.
6. Allow the sauce to cool and pour into a clean siphon suitable for warm preparations. Charge with the specified number of N₂O chargers according to the manufacturer's instructions. Initially shake about 5–6 times, unless the manufacturer specifies otherwise.
7. Refrigerate the filled siphon until needed.
8. Before serving, warm the siphon in a water bath at about 55–60 °C, only if the model is suitable and the manufacturer permits that temperature. Allow enough time for the contents to warm evenly.
9. Shake another 2–3 times and test the foam in a small bowl first. It should be airy and warm and hold its shape for a while.

### Scallops

#### Ingredients

- 4 large fresh scallops, roe removed {scale}
- 1 tbsp neutral oil {scale}
- 10 g butter {scale}
- Fine salt
- A little white pepper, optional

#### Instructions

1. Check the scallops and remove any firm side muscle. Slice each horizontally into two round discs. You now have 8 scallop halves: 4 per person.
2. Pat all discs carefully dry with kitchen paper. Season lightly with salt and, if desired, a little white pepper just before searing.
3. Heat a frying pan over medium-high to high heat and add the neutral oil.
4. Place the scallop halves in the hot pan with the best cut surface facing down. Sear for about 45–60 seconds without moving them, until a golden crust forms.
5. Turn carefully and add the butter. Cook for another 20–30 seconds, briefly basting with the butter. Remove from the pan immediately.
6. Cooking time depends on thickness. These times aim for a soft centre and do not guarantee that the scallops are fully cooked. Cook longer if full cooking is desired.

### Garnish and Plating

#### Ingredients

- 12–16 pomegranate seeds {scale}
- A few small coriander leaves, cress or finely chopped chives
- A few drops of browned cooking butter, optional

#### Instructions

1. Check that the pomegranate seeds are fresh and juicy. Keep chilled until plating. Do not add extra lime juice: the foam already provides enough freshness.
2. Lightly preheat two shallow, bowl-shaped plates.
3. Dispense a round layer of warm coconut foam about 8–10 cm across in the centre of each plate. Use sparingly so the scallops do not sink into it.
4. Carefully arrange 4 scallop halves per person on the foam in a loose, asymmetrical composition, keeping the golden surfaces visible.
5. Scatter 6–8 pomegranate seeds per person between and around the scallops. Add a few small cress or coriander leaves, or a little finely chopped chives.
6. If desired, finish each plate with 2–3 small drops of browned cooking butter for extra flavour and a golden contrast.
7. Serve immediately. The foam looks best just after dispensing.

## Notes

- Preparation takes about 30 minutes, excluding cooling and reheating. Allow extra time for those stages.

### Dinner schedule

**1–2 hours ahead**

- Prepare the sake, lemongrass and coconut sauce, strain finely and season.
- Fill the siphon, charge according to the manufacturer's instructions and chill.
- Prepare the pomegranate seeds.

**Shortly before the starter**

- Bring the siphon to serving temperature according to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Lightly preheat the plates.
- Slice the scallops horizontally in half and pat dry.
- Test the foam.

**Final 3 minutes**

- Sear the scallops briefly over high heat.
- Dispense warm coconut foam onto both plates and arrange four scallop halves on each.
- Garnish with pomegranate and cress and serve immediately.

### Key points

- Keep coconut milk and lime juice restrained so the delicate scallops remain the focus.
- Always strain the foam very finely to prevent the siphon from clogging.
- Never place a pressurised siphon over direct heat or in an excessively hot water bath. Follow its manual for permitted fill levels, chargers and temperatures; do not automatically scale the number of chargers with the recipe.
- Use foam sparingly and dry the scallops thoroughly for a golden crust.
- Add pomegranate and garnish only just before serving.
