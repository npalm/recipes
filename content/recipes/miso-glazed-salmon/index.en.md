Buttery soft baked salmon with a deep brown miso-mirin caramel glaze, served on delicate soba noodles, crunchy stir-fried vegetables and a nutty peanut-cashew topping.

## Components

### Miso Glaze

#### Ingredients
- 1 generous tbsp miso (white/shiro or red) {scale}
- 2 tbsp mirin {scale}
- 1 tbsp sake {scale}
- 1 tbsp soy sauce {scale}
- 1 tbsp finely chopped palm sugar {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Mix the miso, mirin, sake, soy sauce and palm sugar in a saucepan.
2. Stir with a whisk until the miso is completely dissolved.
3. Bring to a boil over low heat and simmer gently for about 2 minutes until the glaze is glossy and slightly syrupy. Turn off the heat.

### Salmon

#### Ingredients
- 2 fresh salmon fillets (approx. 150 g each) {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (top heat or grill setting).
2. Place the salmon fillets in a baking dish lined with baking paper.
3. Generously brush the top with about half of the miso glaze.
4. Place high in the oven for 8 to 10 minutes until the glaze is nicely caramelized and the salmon remains soft and translucent inside.

### Soba Noodles

#### Ingredients
- 120–150 g fresh soba noodles {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
2. Put the fresh soba noodles in a sieve or colander and rinse them thoroughly twice with the boiling water to remove excess flour and heat them up. Drain well.

### Stir-fried Vegetables

#### Ingredients
- Small piece of broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 red sweet pointed pepper, cut into thin strips {scale}
- ½ zucchini, cut into half moons or thin strips {scale}
- A few mushrooms, thinly sliced
- 1 piece of fresh ginger (about the size of half a thumb), cut into fine julienne {scale}
- 1 handful of fresh cilantro (stems finely chopped, leaves coarsely chopped separately) {scale}
- 1 tsp sesame oil {scale}
- 1 tsp fish sauce {scale}
- 1 to 2 tsp fresh lime juice {scale}
- 1 tbsp neutral oil for stir-frying {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat.
2. Stir-fry the pointed pepper, zucchini, mushrooms, ginger strips and finely chopped cilantro stems for 2 to 3 minutes until al dente.
3. Add the broccoli florets and stir-fry for 1 minute.
4. Finally, add the rest of the miso glaze, 1 tsp fish sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil and 1 to 2 tsp lime juice to the hot wok. Let it bubble for 10 seconds.
5. Toss the warm, drained soba noodles through (or turn off the heat and toss through the sauce) so that the glaze coats evenly.

### Plating

#### Ingredients
- Leftover roasted peanuts and cashew nuts (coarsely chopped)

#### Instructions
1. Twist a nest of noodles and stir-fried vegetables in the middle of deep plates.
2. Place the glazed salmon fillet on top.
3. Finish with fresh cilantro leaves and the coarsely chopped nut crunch.
