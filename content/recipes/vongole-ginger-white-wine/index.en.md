A refined Japanese-inspired starter with fresh clams, sake, ginger and a delicate miso sauce made with their own cooking juices.

## Ingredients

- 500 g fresh vongole (clams) {scale}
- 15 g fresh ginger {scale}
- 1 spring onion {scale}
- 1 clove garlic {scale}
- 75 ml sake {scale}
- 1 tbsp neutral oil {scale}
- 1 tsp white miso (shiro miso), plus a little more to taste if needed {scale}
- 10 g cold butter {scale}
- A little finely sliced spring onion for garnish, optional

## Instructions

1. Prepare the clams: Rinse the clams thoroughly under cold water. Discard broken shells and any that do not close when tapped.
2. Prepare the aromatics: Peel the ginger and cut it into very fine strips. Finely slice the spring onion and finely chop the garlic.
3. Cook the aromatics: Heat the oil in a large pan. Gently fry the spring onion for 2 minutes. Add the ginger and garlic and cook for another minute without browning.
4. Steam the clams with sake: Turn the heat up high. Add the clams and sake. Cover immediately and steam for about 3–5 minutes. Shake the pan occasionally until the shells open.
5. Reserve the cooking juices: Lift the opened clams out of the pan and keep them warm briefly. Discard any clams that remain closed. Strain the cooking juices through a fine sieve.
6. Make the miso sauce: Take about 75–100 ml of the strained cooking juices. Reduce briefly if the flavour is too weak. First loosen the white miso with a little warm cooking liquid, then stir it into the sauce. Heat gently without boiling vigorously.
7. Finish with butter: Remove the pan from the heat and whisk the cold butter into the sauce to thicken it slightly and give it a glossy finish. Taste the sauce. Only add extra miso if needed, as the cooking juices are naturally salty.
8. Serve: Divide the clams between two warm deep plates. Spoon the warm miso sauce over and between the shells. Finish with a little finely sliced spring onion if desired.

## Notes

- The sake provides a subtle, aromatic base.
- The ginger adds freshness and a gentle spicy note.
- White miso enhances the umami flavour of the clams.
- Use the cooking juices as the sauce base rather than adding stock.
- Add the miso towards the end and do not let the sauce boil vigorously.
- Serve immediately to keep the clams juicy.
