A light and elegant dessert where citrus-marinated strawberries contrast perfectly with an airy cloud of white chocolate foam from a cream whipper.

## Components

### White Chocolate Foam
prepTime: 10
cookTime: 5
waitTime: 120

#### Ingredients

- 80 g good-quality white chocolate {scale}
- 160 ml heavy cream, min. 35% fat {scale}
- 1 N2O charger

#### Instructions

1. Melt the white chocolate in a bain-marie (double boiler).

2. Warm a small splash of the cream and whisk it into the chocolate to make a smooth ganache.

3. Gradually pour in the remaining **cold** cream, stirring constantly until completely smooth.

4. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a 0.5 L cream whipper.

5. Secure the head, charge with **1** N2O cartridge and shake vigorously {{5-8 times}}.

6. Chill the whipper on its side in the refrigerator for at least {{1-2 hours}}.

### Lime-Marinated Strawberries
prepTime: 10
waitTime: 15

#### Ingredients

- 200 g fresh strawberries, halved {scale}
- 1 organic lime, fine zest and 1 tbsp juice {scale}
- Small pinch of sugar, or a drop of honey {scale}

#### Instructions

1. Combine the lime juice, zest and sugar (or honey) in a small bowl and let it sit briefly so the sugar dissolves.

2. About {{15 minutes}} before serving, toss the halved strawberries through the lime syrup.

### Assembly

#### Instructions

1. Divide the marinated strawberries between two glasses.

2. Remove the whipper from the fridge and give it 2 more vigorous shakes.

3. Test-dispense a small dot of foam onto a saucer to check texture.

4. Holding the whipper completely upside down, pipe the foam over the strawberries and serve immediately.

## Notes

- The whipper must rest **on its side or upside down** in the fridge so the cream stays in contact with the gas, otherwise the foam will be runny.
- Don't overdose the gas; one cartridge is enough for 0.5 L. A second one makes the foam stiff and chalky.
- Substitute raspberries or stone fruit for the strawberries when out of season.
- A few drops of yuzu or finger lime in place of regular lime gives a more elegant edge.
