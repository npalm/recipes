An elegant and fresh appetizer with a deep umami flavor from caramelized mushrooms, the freshness of marinated cucumber, and a crispy nut crunch.

## Components

### Nut Crunch

#### Ingredients
- 1 handful of peanuts and/or cashews, roughly chopped {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Roughly chop the peanuts and cashews.
2. Toast them in a dry skillet over medium-high heat until golden brown and fragrant. Remove immediately from the pan and set aside.

### Marinated Cucumber

#### Ingredients
- ½ cucumber {scale}
- A few drops of lemon or lime juice
- Pinch of salt

#### Instructions
1. Using a vegetable peeler or cheese slicer, shave 4 to 6 thin ribbons lengthwise from the cucumber (stop at the watery seed core).
2. Place the ribbons on a plate, drizzle with a few drops of citrus and a pinch of salt. Let rest until they are soft and pliable.

### Mushroom Tartare

#### Ingredients
- 150 g mushrooms, very finely diced (2–3 mm brunoise) {scale}
- 1 handful of fresh cilantro (stems very finely chopped, leaves chopped separately - use half of the leaves here) {scale}
- 1 tbsp soy sauce {scale}
- 1 tbsp sake {scale}
- 1 tsp palm sugar {scale}
- 1 tbsp neutral oil or sesame oil {scale}

#### Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a skillet over high heat.
2. Briskly fry the finely diced mushrooms together with the cilantro stems for 3 minutes until the moisture has evaporated and they turn golden brown.
3. Add the soy sauce, sake, and palm sugar. Let it reduce over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes until a glossy, syrupy layer coats the mushrooms.
4. Remove from heat, stir in half of the chopped cilantro leaves, and let it cool slightly to lukewarm.

### Dressing & Plating

#### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp neutral oil {scale}
- A few drops of lemon or lime juice
- 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh chives {scale}
- Remaining fresh cilantro leaves

#### Instructions
1. Mix the rest of the cilantro leaves with 1 tbsp of oil and a few drops of citrus to make a quick herb oil.
2. Place a plating ring on the plate and line the inside tightly with a marinated cucumber ribbon.
3. Fill the ring with the lukewarm mushroom tartare and press down lightly. Carefully remove the ring.
4. Garnish the top generously with the chives and the toasted nut crunch.
5. Playfully drizzle the herb oil around the tartare on the plate.
